    How scoring works

    Each queen is worth up to 100 points. The sooner you find it after the previous queen, the more it's worth.

    The points for a puzzle are multiplied by its difficulty: Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2 and Expert ×3. Difficulty depends on how tricky the reasoning is, not just the board size.

    Then they're multiplied by your streak. Every puzzle you solve without help adds one to your streak, up to ×10.

    Using Hint or Solve 1, losing, or leaving a puzzle unfinished resets your streak. Queens placed for you earn no points, and replaying a puzzle you've already solved adds nothing.
